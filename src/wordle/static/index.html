    How it works

    Every word stays possible

    All 14,855 valid guesses are candidates, weighted by how common they are in real English text. Obscure words become unlikely, never impossible.

    Guess for information

    Each guess is scored by its entropy: how evenly its possible colour patterns split the remaining probability. An even split teaches the most.

    Look two steps ahead

    The top 50 guesses are re-scored by adding the best follow-up's information for every outcome. With one word dominating, it simply goes for the win.
